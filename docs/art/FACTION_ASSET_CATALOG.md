# Faction asset coverage

All twelve authored cultures have 18 distinct, individually approved role assets each: 15 historical land/UI roles plus transport, coastal warship and ocean warship. Generated campaign seats inherit their definition's kit, not a name-based reskin. These 216 static southeast first-facing assets are published for actual map and UI consumers; they are not full directional/action animation or twenty-four completed cultures. The prior 36 naval gaps are now filled by qualified hulls, with ship-shaped procedural fallbacks retained for missing/failed loads. Historical land-gallery acceptance and current integration gates remain separately recorded in [art status](ART_IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md).
