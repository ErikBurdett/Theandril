## Limits

These deliberately authored map galleries use grassland and shallow-water lanes with normal fog and imported validated states; they do not demonstrate every culture against every biome or every kind of ocean. Bright original terrain variants still create visible patchwork and busy ground, especially behind dark troops; biome transitions and complete palette unification remain unfinished. Most faction art is static: 431 static qualified assets and one four-frame Ashen scout idle. Full action/directional animation, feeding, life-steal, automatic amphibious travel, flight and tunneling are not granted by artwork.
