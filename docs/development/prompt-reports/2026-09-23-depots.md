# Depots: building supply instead of conquering it — 2026-09-23

## Prompt

“continue”, taking the next slice of M4. Rules 27 gave supply a shape; this gives a realm a way to extend it that does not require taking someone else's hearth.

## What was missing

Supply followed hearths, and hearths came from founding or conquest. A realm could reach further by building a road, but only along ground it already held, and an army put ashore overseas or pushed into open country had no way to be fed short of founding a town. The one lever the system offered — take the next hearth — was the same lever the system was supposed to make expensive.

## Delivered — rules/save 28

- **A company raises a depot where it stands.** It spends its movement and forty coin, and the depot feeds two hexes of open country around it, or four along a road — half a hearth's reach, seeded into the same search.
- **Depots cost to keep.** Two coin a turn, charged with every other obligation, so a network of them is a real line in the budget rather than a free map.
- **They stand apart.** Depots of one realm keep three hexes between them, and a realm may hold thirty-two, so reach is a network of posts rather than a carpet.
- **They are pulled down.** Any enemy company that walks onto a depot razes it at the end of the turn, and both realms are told. A supply network is now a thing to raid and a thing to defend.
- **Refusals are plain, and the UI gives them in the same order the rules do.** An embarked army, a spent company, an empty purse, a hearth already on that ground, a depot already there, a neighbour's claim, then the spacing rule — one sentence each, and the button names the price.

## Not done

- The AI does not build depots and does not raid them. It is not yet worse off for that, because it rarely campaigns far enough from its hearths to need one.
- Ports do not exist: fleets still carry their own stores, and a landing still has to raise a depot rather than draw on a harbour across the water.
- Depots are only supply. They hold nothing, garrison nothing and have no defences of their own — anything hostile that reaches one takes it down.
- There is still no map overlay for supply reach, so a depot's effect is read from the selected force rather than seen.
