# Standing postings and muster points: M3's other half — 2026-09-22

## Prompt

“continue”, taking the next item on the road to 1.0. Rules 25 delivered M3's settlement half — a standing production charter per hearth. This is the army half: *coordinated orders* rather than one command per company per turn.

## What was missing

Every army order named exactly one army. A company completed at a hearth arrived as its own one-formation army, and the production panel said so plainly: *“Each company arrives as a separate detachment. Assign a healthy marshal and use Army composition to combine co-located formations.”* The game was telling the player to do, by hand and every time, the one thing it could obviously do for them. A realm raising companies at six hearths spent most of its turn marching singletons toward a front and merging them on arrival.

## Delivered — rules/save 26

- **A posting is a standing travel order.** An army may be posted to a hex it has explored. Each turn it marches there under the ordinary travel order — same pathing, same visibility, same hostile-sighting pause — and then either **holds** the hex or **joins** the force standing on it.
- **It never fights a real order.** A posting only starts a march for an army with no travel order of its own: an active route runs, a paused route keeps its reason, and the posting waits. Clearing it returns the army to direct orders.
- **Arriving and joining are the same turn.** An army that reaches its posting merges that turn rather than standing idle beside the force it was sent to, under the ordinary command-capacity rules.
- **A hearth can name a muster point.** Every company it raises is posted there the turn it forms and joins what it finds. Set it once and the singleton-marching disappears; clear it and companies stay where they are raised.
- **It always says where it stands.** *Marching to its posting*, *Standing at its posting*, the paused route's own reason, *No safe route to the posting is known*, *No other force of this realm stands here to join*, or *The army is aboard a fleet* — derived on demand, so nothing is stale in a save, a replay or the chronicle.
- **Attention follows.** An army under a working posting leaves the next-action list; a stalled one appears as *“Posting stalled: …”* with the posting's own reason. Together with rules 25's charters, both halves of a turn's busywork now answer for themselves and only exceptions ask for a decision.
- **Refusals are plain.** A posting to an unexplored hex is refused outright; a hearth or army that changes hands loses its order at the next turn rather than serving a conqueror.

## Not done

- A posting names one hex. There are no theatre orders, no patrol or escort roles, and no way to post several armies at once.
- Alerts are filtered and explained but still not grouped by cause: forty stalled postings are forty rows.
- The AI does not use postings; it already merges co-located armies each turn and gains nothing from a standing order it re-derives anyway.
