## What it says

- The Epic pace is seed-dependent across a range wider than its band: the
  spread between the fastest and slowest of nine courses is 163 turns, and
  the band holds a third of them.
- The headline seed's 329 is within the lower cluster, not an outlier; the
  mean of nine sits inside the band. No single rule since rules 63 moved
  every seed one way: the catalogues redraw the fortunes, the figures and
  offices redraw the roads, and each course diverges on its own.
- Whether the Epic band should be re-priced — a wider band, a nominal
  target with a stated spread, or a longer pace — with the current pack
  frozen is the owner's decision, as rules 18, 21 and 22 were. Until it is
  taken, the headline seed is reported against the band as it stands and
  the drift stays recorded.

No rules, content or AI changed for this survey; no deploy accompanies it.
