The group-travel slice adds explicit coordinated journeys: Group travel reviews compact canonical routes for up to 128 selected owned land armies ashore, then explicitly applies replacement routes or appended waypoints in stable army-ID order through ordinary commands. Current movement can be spent immediately. Resume and cancel act on eligible selected routes; existing postings remain and can resume their own movement later unless separately cleared. Accepted armies leave the selection; refused armies remain for correction. Preview context is hash-bound and advisory, with individual overrides and exact saved/replayed outcomes. Uncertain mutation responses lock orders until a saved campaign is restored; superseded preview errors do not lock a restored campaign. Theaters, patrol/escort roles, reusable army-order templates, broader governor decisions and combined mature-realm acceptance remain open. This slice does not add synchronized arrival, automatic attacks/war, standing repeat production or automatic retries.
- M4 in-progress — Supply, trade and sustained military operations: Land, depot and harbor supply plus finite fleet provisions are published under rules/save 31. - M5 in-progress — Independent powers, political pressure and agent roles: Twenty-four city-states are authored, playable and courtable as clients. Clans, pirates, mercenaries, enclaves and the political layer are not built.
- M6 open — Epochs, crises and the living campaign: Navigate eras, upheaval and world crises rather than a flat timeline.
- M7 open — Progression depth and the third victory path: Two victory paths exist; the third and the deeper progression behind it do not.
