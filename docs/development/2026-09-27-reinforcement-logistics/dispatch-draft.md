## Draft article

A standing watch can keep a hearth staffed. It now has a separate setting for a visible threat: allow up to four extra guards when enemy armies approach. With one extra guard enabled, a watch holding its ordinary garrison can send an idle assigned reserve toward a threatened hearth. The realm's direct routes and postings keep priority, and the watch cannot strip another hearth below its required physical coverage. Each visible wartime combat land army within three hexes requests one extra guard, up to the chosen limit. An army means a whole container here; this setting does not estimate the relative strength of the opposing forces.

Additional coverage persists through one quiet allocation phase, so losing sight of a contact does not immediately pull the reserve back. Hidden armies, neutral forces, caravans and embarked passengers do not trigger reinforcement. Existing watches migrate with the option off. Newly established AI watches can choose one extra guard and up to three affordable spare armies while preserving scouts and the two strongest field armies. The AI still does not rewrite or refill an established watch. Theater work remains bounded to sixteen ordinary route attempts per theater per turn, and failed or interrupted travel remains visible.

For an expedition beyond its own supply line, the new diplomatic option is more direct. Choose a currently observed foreign hearth or harbor, review a fee and a term, and send a request. The ordinary quote is two coin per turn: twenty coin for ten turns. The request lasts three turns; payment happens once when the provider accepts. A functioning contracted harbor can feed ships and their passengers through the existing supply rules, while its land reach can support a nearby field force. The agreement does not move an army, grant passage or change the rules for war.

The retained buyer journey starts with an explicitly prepared overseas force and a fleet at empty stores. Its twenty-coin request is accepted by the provider AI on End turn. Fleet, passengers and the field army draw supplies from Reedwatch Quay; the fleet returns to eight provision turns. Ending the agreement then leaves seven turns aboard ship and costs each unsupported land formation four strength on the following turn. Negotiating again restores the supply line and stops further land losses. The saved campaign and its command record reopen exactly. A separate keyboard journey accepts an incoming request as the provider and verifies one payment.

This is a purchase of service from one named source. Either party can end it without a refund; war, loss of the source and expiry can end it too. The map shows last-known contracted coverage from explored terrain, remembered roads and visible blockers. An army's own supply report says whether it is actually being fed. A hidden blockade can interrupt delivery without secretly redrawing the map or revealing where the blocker stands. Treaty names, locations and contracted harbor capability remain the facts disclosed when the offer was made.

The AI considers a purchase for observed forces that need supplies, uses the same public assessment as the player, and reserves promised fees before discretionary spending. It checks at most two actors and four foreign sources, with at most eight ordinary route previews. An equally near or nearer known supply cell conservatively vetoes an optional purchase. One request per ten-turn faction cadence, no second outgoing request while one is pending, and a protected requesting force keep this behavior narrow. This is support for a legal resupply opportunity, not evidence of a complete overseas strategy.

Joining these systems is the other part of the work. An authored Huge realm contains 128 owned armies, thirty hearths and two wars. Its browser exercise combines grouped exceptions, policies, a finite production budget, saved selections, reviewed travel, theaters, direct overrides and saved continuation. Enabled theater members no longer flood the ordinary idle-army queue; grouped theater attention opens the relevant watch. The completed journey preserves its exact AI-inclusive future through saved restoration and portable import. It found a keyboard-focus ordering defect, now corrected. Its size and disclosed threat isolation are a deliberate workload, not a claim that the AI organically built that realm.

Rules/save 34 adds reinforcement policy and holds plus the supply agreement register. Before those changes, ten genuine rules 33 save/archive checkpoints and twenty-seven independently repeated accepted commands were captured. Seven continuations retain their historical results. Migration supplies empty modern defaults, and historical projection refuses to discard new obligations or consumed identifiers. Ordinary commands remain the only authority for payments and movement. If an accepted payment cannot be safely published to the client, gameplay locks for saved restoration instead of allowing a stale screen to repeat the transaction.
