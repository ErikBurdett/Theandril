## Finding and correction

`packages/ai/src/index.ts` calls `planDiplomacy(view)` before subtracting `pendingSupplyAccessFees(view)`. Consequently the early diplomacy return can spend the purse promised to a pending supply provider. For example, with 20 coin and a pending 20-coin service offer to one realm, a prolonged pressured war with another realm can make a 5-coin incoming peace reparation score as likely. `planDiplomacy` sees the full 20; `respondPeace` immediately leaves 15, making the promised service fee unpayable. The existing ordinary-spending and victory-project reservation tests do not exercise this early return.

Reported to the parent and supply/AI author. The parent subsequently moved pending-fee reservation before discretionary diplomacy. The independent follow-up source read confirms the detached purse now reaches peace and patronage decisions, and the new supply AI regression creates an actual third-realm promise and paid peace offer before verifying that acceptance cannot consume the fee. The retained peace-reservation focused log reports all ten supply AI tests passing. This finding is closed by that correction and regression; no production file was changed by this reviewer.

## Source findings with no additional blocker

- `packages/sim/src/theater-state.ts` keeps a strict rules-33 schema/command boundary, requires policy and holds in version-34 saves, bounds the limit to 0–4, and validates sorted distinct protected-hearth holds against enabled policy and the current turn. Old-rule execution/serialization rejects nonzero reinforcement state rather than silently discarding it.
- `packages/sim/src/theaters.ts` reads actual visible wartime combat land armies within radius three. Carried armies, naval forces, founders, unseen units and remembered contacts do not increase demand. This is a whole-army count, not a prediction of enemy strength or battle outcome.
- The allocation index distinguishes stationary armies from active incoming routes. An incoming promise never licenses withdrawing the physical minimum from another protected hearth. Incoming routes reduce the remaining deficit without proving arrival. Any active or paused direct route, posting, siege, carried state, or character mission blocks automatic reassignment. Existing routes remain after pause, detach or delete.
- Reinforcement demand is capped, retained through the next quiet allocation, then expires. Lowering policy clamps retained extras; disabling clears holds. The allocator retains the existing stable rotation and 16-attempt cap per theater, including refused route attempts. It issues ordinary queue-movement commands and does not attack automatically.
- `apps/web/src/defense-theaters.tsx` submits the capability-gated policy explicitly, preserves it during pause/detach, describes visible enemies and the quiet hold, and reads canonical report counts. `theater-response.ts` correlates the accepted policy with the submitted edit. `main.tsx` keeps the existing response validation/recovery boundary and clears theater focus when a campaign is replaced.
- Attention navigation consumes canonical missing/refused/unavailable reports, opens the army registry, selects the named theater and focuses its summary. Healthy delegated members are removed only from idle-work hints; starvation, interrupted travel and stalled postings still remain actionable.
- `packages/ai/src/theaters.ts` opts current campaigns into one extra guard while retaining the historical two-spare policy for rules 33. Adoption remains bounded by four candidate hearths and eight permitted route reviews; current campaigns can assign at most three spares. Scouts, direct commitments and the two strongest ordinary field containers remain reserved.
- Active supply partners are protected bilaterally in `protectedFactions`; pending providers are protected for the buyer. A newly proposed provider is also added to the current pass's land and naval target exclusions. The requesting actor is held out of subsequent ordinary movement/reorganization, and the new fee is removed from the detached planning purse. Existing pending fees are reserved for diplomacy and ordinary progression/production after the correction above. These are AI policies, not new binding truces in canonical diplomacy.
