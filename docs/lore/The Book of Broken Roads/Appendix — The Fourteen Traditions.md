> Magic is not a thing men have. It is a thing places and bloodlines and bargains leak. — hedge-teaching, all ages

For readers of the coming age who will meet these powers on the field, at the council table, or in their own children: the springs of magic as twenty-three centuries of records attest them. A tradition is an *inheritance* — an aptitude carried in blood, place, or pact, determining what a given soul can wield. A discipline is a *knowledge* — what a realm's scholars have worked out about wielding it. The two are forever confused by the ignorant and forever separated in practice: a realm may hold deep theory no living subject can perform, and a marsh-child may carry aptitude no school has words for. Every catastrophe in this book except the honest ones began in the gap between those two.

## The fourteen springs

- **Flame** — destruction, heat, the forge, purification. The Kiln faith's darling; the Ashfall's suspect.
- **Storm** — lightning, wind, weather, the high air. League pilots married into it for generations, deliberately.
- **Tide** — water, cold, navigation, the sea's consent. The Glass Tide's coasts are thick with the aptitude and poor, so far, in its schooling.
- **Stone** — earth, endurance, fortification, metal in the ground. The Anvilheights' other treasury.
- **Verdancy** — growth, beasts, healing, the wild. The Deepfen's first estate; distrusted by every besieger who has watched a wall hedge itself overnight.
- **Grave** — death, the housed dead, decay and its arrest. My own condition's spring. Feared everywhere, employed everywhere, honest about neither.
- **Star** — divination, distance, portals, fate's paperwork. The Synod's far-writing and the League's navigation both drew here; so, some claim, did the Roads.
- **Dream** — illusion, sleep, morale, the inward country. The Covenant bred it out and boasted; the reader has seen what a folk that cannot lie to itself did anyway.
- **Shadow** — concealment, curses, the unwatched act. Every crown in this book kept Shadow servants and no crown ever admitted it in a treaty I sealed.
- **Radiance** — wards, revelation, the sacred fire. The exorcists' spring; the Hollowing made its practitioners wealthy and haunted in equal measure.
- **Spirit** — ancestors, possession, pact and shamanry. The old hearth religion entire; the Roads' folk-doctrine lives here.
- **Rune** — binding, inscription, artifice, the made thing that works. The Covenant's glory; the null-anchors' spring, which the reader should sit with.
- **Oath** — bond, lineage, sacrifice, the coercive sanctities. The spring of the stones themselves, by my order's doctrine; the spring of soul-collateral, by demonstrated fact. Handle accordingly.
- **Void** — the outside power. I record of it only this: every age's records mention it, no age's records explain it, and the gaps in several archives I have copied are themselves the testimony. Where the reader finds a burned page in this world, read the burning.

## The nine disciplines

The old schools organized their knowledge nine ways, and the new schools are already rediscovering the same shelves: **Calling**, which summons and binds; **Transmutation**, which changes body, matter, and land; **Projection**, which makes the springs into weapons at distance; **Warding**, which shields, dispels, and nullifies — the Throne's whole genius; **Artifice**, which fixes power into things; **Veilcraft**, which deceives — the counterfeit seals were Veilcraft, and the reader has seen the invoice; **Malison**, which curses and afflicts; **Thaumic Dominion**, which works at the scale of weather, realm, and age, and stands accused by half my sources of the Ashfall itself; and the **Forbidden Arts**, which are forbidden, and which every power in this book kept a locked drawer of anyway, and which made the hand that writes this sentence. The reader will find no hypocrisy in me on the last point. Only the record, and the request that the coming age read it before opening the drawer.

> A last note for the practical. Aptitude without knowledge is a child with a coal. Knowledge without aptitude is a library in a language no one speaks. Realms rise on the match between the two — and fall, this book attests from end to end, on the mismatch. Count your casters before you covet your neighbor's theory. — I.V.
