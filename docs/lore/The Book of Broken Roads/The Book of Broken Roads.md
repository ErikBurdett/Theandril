> Whether the builders of the Witness Roads spoke to the dead or merely recorded them, I cannot tell you — and I was there.

I am Ilthen Vael, once Third Recorder of the waystation at Grey Weir, struck from the rolls of the Order of Witnesses in the year 2231 of the Road Reckoning for the crime of refusing to die. The ragged folk of the hearthlands call me **Ledgerbone**, and leave tallow candles at my door in winter, which is kind and useless. I do not eat. I do not sleep. I write.

I took the Second Interment — the rite the living call *lichdom*, spitting sideways as they say it — for one reason, and I set it here at the front of the book so no reader mistakes me: **records die with their recorders.** Parchment rots. Stone shears. Memory flatters. A witness who cannot die is the only archive that keeps itself. I chose to become a dead thing that remembers, and I have never once regretted it, and the price I paid is written in [[V. The Recorder's Bargain]] where it belongs, as an entry like any other.

> [!note]
> **The chronicler's vow.** This book records events, not motives. Where I saw a thing, I say so. Where I copied a seal, I name the seal. Where accounts conflict, I give the conflict and do not resolve it. A history that claims to know why men did what they did is not a history; it is a eulogy, or an indictment. The Witnessry taught me that much before it cast me out, and it is the one oath of theirs I never broke.

What follows is the history of the world of **Theandril** from the unwritten dark to the present hour — the hour in which new banners rise over the rekindled hearths, and maps and promises have become valuable again. It ends where your own record must begin.

## The order of the book

1. [[I. The Unwritten World]] — what came before records, and why I will not invent it.
2. [[II. The Age of First Oaths]] — the hearth peoples, the fourteen springs of magic, and the First Sealing at Cold Ford.
3. [[III. The Raising of the Roads]] — how the Witness Roads were built, what they did, and what they cost.
4. [[IV. The Age of Crowns]] — the five great powers of the old world, and the rot that ripened under the gold.
5. [[V. The Recorder's Bargain]] — my mortal life, my death, and the terms of my continuing.
6. [[VI. The Reckoning]] — the wars that broke the world: the War of Written Fire, the Severing, the Hollowing.
7. [[VII. The Failing and the Ashfall]] — the two years in which the Roads went quiet and the sky came down.
8. [[VIII. The Long Ash]] — one hundred and thirty-four years of ruin, and what crawled out of it.
9. [[IX. The Rekindled Hearths]] — the four powers of the present hour: Compact, Council, March, and Tide.
10. [[X. The Present Hour]] — the world as it stands in RR 2447, at the dawn of a new Age of Fracture.
11. [[Appendix — The Fourteen Traditions]] — the springs of magic and the nine disciplines, for readers who will need them.

*On reckoning: I count years from the First Sealing at Cold Ford — the Road Reckoning, written RR. The Roads stood for two thousand three hundred and eleven years. They have been quiet for one hundred and thirty-six. The living have begun counting again from their own little foundings, each hearthland with its own calendar, which is how you know an age has truly broken.*
