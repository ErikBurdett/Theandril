# Twelve hearthlands, not twelve versions of one kingdom

This development appendix adds regional powers to the playable roster without rewriting the Book of Broken Roads. The Book remains authoritative about witnessed events: the Mire Courts endured, the old Saltwind League broke, and the Null Throne fell. The two Remnants below are successor organizations, not retroactive reversals of those events. Rimehorn, Sable and Morrow are newly authored societies beyond the Book's principal political account; their names do not claim previously attested empires.

The [out-of-world faction bible](FACTION_BIBLE.md) is the expanded reference for all twelve current cultures: their origins, material identities, relationships, tensions and lore-only character seeds. Its second part proposes twelve further cultures, explicitly unregistered, not playable and not art-complete. This cohort remains the historical six-to-twelve addition, not evidence that the proposed twenty-four are available.

## The six additions

A broken-keel seal, distinct from Glass Tide's coastal imagery. |
| Wardhall Remnant | Human work halls preserve engineering discipline while disputing whether reconstruction needs royal authority. An open-square seal. |
| Rimehorn Clans | Human and giantkin shelter households share winter stores. Seasonal gatherings arbitrate distant clans' claims without automatically subordinating every hearth to a permanent crown. | Cold-country provisions and timber work favor guards, spears and durable infantry. ## Implemented identity and future depth

The authoritative numbers live in [ecology](../../packages/content/src/ecology.ts) and [factions](../../packages/content/src/factions.ts), not this prose. Bonuses and penalties affect worked-tile yields; cultivation consumes the existing scaling costs and work time. Affinity does not remove movement costs or grant territorial military access. Every culture can recruit the current common units; the listed army preferences influence the AI's paid recruitment, not player restrictions or free troops. Characters retain distinct deterministic name pools and the existing appointment and skill rules.

The Mire Courts' Verdancy/Spirit/Dream heritage, old maritime Tide/Storm/Star traditions and Wardhall's anti-magic inheritance remain lore and future progression hooks. No caster, nullification, supernatural logistics or automatic fungal expansion is represented as implemented by costume or a description. Future asymmetric rosters and research need their own canonical commands, costs, AI use, saved continuation and browser evidence.

This cohort raises the playable definition count from six to twelve toward the requested twenty-four. A campaign may contain more seats than distinct definitions; repeated seats receive explicit names and keep a canonical culture ID. Such repeated seats are not additional unique factions. Art qualification separately requires all fifteen static land-unit, character, settlement and heraldic roles per culture; planned briefs are not approved artwork, and naval hull variants remain a separate gap.
