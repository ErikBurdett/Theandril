Worked-land strengths & drawbacks

Temperate grassland: +1 food per worked tile
Temperate forest: +1 industry per worked tile
Tundra: −1 food per worked tile
Desert: −1 food per worked tile

Unlisted biomes are neutral. These contributions modify worked tiles, not movement or combat.

Cultivation traditions: Temperate grassland, Temperate forest. Cultivation is paid work, not free conversion.
