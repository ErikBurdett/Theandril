Rule from the map

Select a force, then choose Move on map and click a reachable hex or enemy. A distant destination opens a route review before any order is issued. Shift-click adds waypoints. Journeys pause when danger, another faction, or an event blocks them.

Click a settlement to build or recruit. Click its surrounding land to inspect yields, improve a tile, or grow its borders. All prices and restrictions come from the current campaign.

The floating buttons open your rosters, research, characters, diplomacy and journal. The bottom tray follows your selected army or town. Full orders provide the same controls in a larger window.

Navigation & shortcuts

Drag to pan; scroll or ± to zoom. World overview fits the entire map. Arrow keys pan when the map is focused. Enter opens local actions. Escape closes the current window before clearing the map selection.

N next army · S next idle settlement · Shift + shortcut selects the previous entry. Active journeys, siege duty, embarked troops and stationary missions are skipped; interrupted routes still need review.

Change text size and key bindings in Campaign & settings. Map click menus can be disabled; full orders and keyboard navigation remain available.
