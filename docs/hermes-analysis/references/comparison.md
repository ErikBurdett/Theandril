# Premium comparison principles — not a cloning brief

This is a **design comparison**, not a fresh playtest, content census or performance benchmark of the reference games. Official descriptions establish their advertised pillars; the quality lessons and Theandril recommendations are editorial judgments tied to this audit's own evidence. A smaller original game can meet a premium bar through coherence, differentiation and consequence; it does not need another studio's cumulative DLC count.

## Dominions: combinations, constraints, experimentation

Illwinter describes a large variety of nations, units, spells and magical items, and different strengths depending on the chosen god and nation.[1]

**Quality lesson / proposal:** depth comes from interacting constraints—who can learn/use a capability, what infrastructure or geography enables it, what scarce commitment it takes, and how an opponent can respond—not simply a long spell list. Experiments should create markedly different strategic plans and surprising but explainable consequences.

**Theandril:** separate national discovery and saved personal aptitude is the right foundation. Real C99 turn37 casts demonstrate it works. But every Waykeeper has the same two aptitudes; two universal battle effects cannot support an independent strategic pillar. Close an original site/resource/practitioner/strategic-effect/counter loop before writing dozens of similar damage spells. Preserve Theandril's own distinction between practical knowledge, Arcane Theory, sacred and occult concepts; do not import Dominions terminology or its spell catalog. See `systems/report.md:140-146`, `campaigns/findings.md:45-49`, R08/R09/R25.

## Civilization: visible progression and understandable opportunities

The official Civilization VI description links city expansion on the map, technology/culture unlocks, leaders with distinct agendas, and alternative ways to win.[5]

**Quality lesson / proposal:** the map should explain investments, the interface should communicate their likely results, and a new player should perceive an attainable next goal. Strategic depth and clarity reinforce each other. 'One more turn' should mean an approaching payoff or emerging dilemma, not just an affordable threshold thousands of coin away.

**Theandril:** real claims, households, paid improvements and expanding settlements provide a strong foothold. Exact blockers and queue prices are useful. Campaign A still left three households unused without an attention item; its journal pushed important decisions behind scout-step noise. A compact attention/forecast layer and better first-campaign teaching have more immediate value than replacing the whole UI. Civic accounts and one economic project are not enough alternative endings. See R04/R05/R07/R14/R16/R20.

## Total War: Warhammer: armies must express faction fantasy

The publisher's description emphasizes different races, broad unit choices and large real-time battle spectacle.[4]

**Quality lesson / proposal:** a player should recognize what a force is, what its roles do, why an army composition matters, and what happened when it won or broke. Campaign choices should have a visible battlefield identity. Spectacle is successful when it explains consequences, not when it merely adds particles or entity count.

**Theandril:** pike/mounted/missile interactions, persistent casualties, commanders and transport losses are genuine. Yet formation order follows creation IDs, legal 21-formation stacks cannot be attacked, and a small observed battle occupies a sparse field. Fix contestability first, then deliberate deployment and refinement of the existing authentic native action clips and terrain context. A deterministic turn-based battle director with strong presentation fits the project; an RTS rewrite is not justified. See R03/R13/R18/R19.

## Songs of Conquest: a distinct aesthetic and a remembered ending

Songs of Conquest's official presentation describes campaign-specific songs whose verses unlock across missions and culminate at the journey's end.[3]

**Quality lesson / proposal:** premium pixel presentation is a complete, consistent language, including framing, animation, audio and milestone treatment—not just isolated attractive sprites. An ending should acknowledge the player's particular journey.

**Theandril:** preserve the pixel world, dark ledger UI, original culture names and factual chronicles. Add original sound design and tiered feedback for discoveries, founding, major battles and victory. Its factual history is a promising identity, but in-progress player history must remain discoverable and the full archive must remain saveable. Do not copy songs, palettes, character designs or assets. See R02/R17/R20/R24 and the art audit.

## Age of Wonders and broader grand-strategy expectations

The Age of Wonders 4 publisher describes choices opening new tactical possibilities and battles shaped by those decisions.[6]

**Quality lesson / proposal:** specialization should change available tools and the problems a player can solve. More additive nodes alone are weaker than unlocks that connect army composition, map investment and opponent adaptation.

Europa Universalis and Stellaris are used here only as **user-requested design lenses** for political obligations, durable strategic goals and late escalation; this audit did not freshly inspect those games or claim comparative counts. For Theandril, a bounded access/exchange/client obligation model and state-triggered regional developments would create real changing objectives. A comprehensive historical-government simulator or galaxy-scale crisis system is not automatically a 1.0 requirement. See R06/R08/R12/R14/R15.

## Bounded premium scope

- **Required:** safe campaigns, contestable warfare, coherent competing strategies, genuinely differentiated released factions, meaningful fantasy magic, capable opponents, usable large empires, complete original presentation and reliable endings.
- **High-value polish:** richer veteran stories, refined milestone framing, extensive cosmetic variants beyond minimum role readability.
- **Post-1.0 proposals unless explicitly retained in the written scope:** unrestricted political life simulation, RTS battles, cinematic campaign movies, huge numbers of new races or redundant currencies. No such rewrite is supported by this audit.
- The written Theandril content/path targets remain unmet. A smaller non-filler equivalent is a **scope proposal requiring explicit adoption**, not something an auditor can silently declare complete.
