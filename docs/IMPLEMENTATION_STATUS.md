## Verified conquest and negotiated peace — slice 3

Players can blockade adjacent hostile towns, reduce their defenses and stores over turns, assault fortified stationed garrisons or empty-town militia through the existing tactical engine, and relieve a siege by defeating its besieger. Blockades suppress real settlement yields. A successful assault pauses for a saved capture choice: occupation, sack, raze, or liberation to a distinct original founder when applicable. Outcomes alter ownership, treasury, population, buildings, production queues, devastation and recovery. Razed settlements leave visible, saved ruins; a normally recruited caravan can resettle them through the founding command.

The negotiation builder reviews complete coin-for-peace packages and returns qualitative reasons and objections. Offers expire after three turns and are limited per pair; payments are rechecked atomically on acceptance, not escrowed. Accepted terms exchange coin, end the war and its blockades, preserve settlement ownership, and prevent a new declaration until the binding truce expires. Pair-level trust/respect/grievances persist; this is not the full relationship/politics system. AI proposes/responds through observations and commands, invests visible towns, chooses assaults and scores capture consequences. Human siege defense pauses correctly; AI capture decisions resolve through fresh command proposals.

Schema 3 preserves pending sieges, tactical rounds, capture choices, ruins and diplomacy. ## Verification

Current known limits: land/shallow/deep routing is bounded to explored geography, 4,096 search nodes and 256 route hexes; there is no hierarchical routing. Sight remains radius-based. There is no economic starvation/population decline outside capture consequences; text scaling is session-local while validated letter shortcuts persist in local browser settings; no server; large registries are not virtualized. Field attacks/assaults initiate with one composed army and support at most twenty actual formations per side; larger defending stacks are explicitly rejected. Peace covers coin and a binding truce, not settlement transfers, alliances, access or client rule. Siege sabotage and field engineers exist, but defenses/stores and empty-town militia remain basic, without naval bombardment, reinforcement orders or civilian migration. Occupation is a timed yield penalty with reconstruction, not full culture/stability/resistance. AI explores frontiers, concentrates formations, uses paid officers/skills/missions and sails real expeditions, but lacks persistent theaters/invasion logistics, full difficulty behavior and long-campaign variety. Local saves commit incremental history chunks; full in-memory journals and complete portable envelopes retain a 64 MiB UTF-8 policy and still need memory/export profiling for 1,000-turn giant mature empires. Oversized saves fail visibly without evicting valid generations; no archive is silently truncated. Technical per-turn UI previews cap exceptional pages at 200,000 characters while downloads retain all data. Checksums detect accidental corruption, not maliciously rewritten history. Production retains non-fatal large-main-chunk and upstream Zod annotation warnings; the final production main is 772.87 kB / 236.40 kB gzip, worker 507.33 kB and CSS 55.62 kB / 11.15 kB gzip. All ten built JavaScript chunks exclude Art Lab/debug hooks. Browser coverage is Chromium 151.0.7922.173 on Arch Linux; Firefox/WebKit remain unverified.
