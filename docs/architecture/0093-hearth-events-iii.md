# ADR 0093 — Rules/save 62: the third hearth-event catalogue

**Status:** accepted, 2026-10-31. Content only; no canonical field. Save 62
freezes the rules 60–61 pack. Evidence:
[`docs/development/2026-10-31-hearth-events-iii/`](../development/2026-10-31-hearth-events-iii/README.md).

## Context

The scope asks for one hundred and fifty hearth events; sixty were sealed
under rules 59, and a realm that plays a long campaign meets the same
fortunes twice. Every condition the catalogue reads — a hearth's harbor,
archive, garrison, youth, breadth, scars, coast, peace or war, the ages of
the world — had three or four events; the rarer ones needed more.

## Decision

- **Fifteen more templates**, across the conditions and the ages: the
  lantern festival, the widows' toll, a harbor-master's offer, the lost
  ledger, muster day, the long peace (Roads), far cousins, the first
  harvest, the cistern silts, a foundling at the gate, a glass pedlar
  (Seams), rumours of the bid (Bids) and the wager that comes due, the tide
  flats, the old walls fall. Seventy-five templates; the rules 61 catalogue
  is frozen.
- **A default that commits to nothing.** Every chain of the catalogue keeps
  its first answer — the one a lapsed event takes — free of a follow-up, as
  the earlier chains did; the wager's default keeps out of it.
- **Save 62** upgrades v61 envelopes unchanged under the new pack.

## Consequences

- Headline pacing under rules 62: Epic 386, Long 305, Standard 214; the two
  other seeds 390 / 362, 295 / 282, 183 / 202; proxies 336 / 440 / 289 / 197:
  every headline course of every seed inside its band, the first full
  matrix in band this session.
- The AI-watch chronicle journey's pin moves 54 → 53, attributed: a draw of
  the larger catalogue lands on another template.
- The hearth-event test now counts only drawn offers toward the cadence
  invariant; a chain's follow-up and a crisis's own event are given, not
  drawn.
