# ADR 0109 — Rules/save 77: four kinds of city-state

**Status:** accepted, 2026-11-01. Content only; no canonical field. Save 77
freezes the rules 74–76 pack. Evidence:
[`docs/development/2026-11-01-city-state-kinds/`](../development/2026-11-01-city-state-kinds/README.md).

## Context

The independent-powers roadmap asks for clans, pirates, nomads and trade
communities with distinct relations, not forty-eight seats of one kind.
The city-states already trade goods (rules 60), pay tribute at the gate
(rules 61) and sell companies there (rules 68); what they sell and what
they pay is where a kind can first show.

## Decision

- **Four kinds**, a table keyed by city-state like the trade goods, so the
  rosters' own objects and their frozen seals are untouched: a market town
  sells line troops at the going rate and pays tribute in full; a clanhold
  sells its oath guard at a quarter more and pays half; a nomad camp sells
  riders at half again and pays three quarters; a pirate cove sells
  skirmishers at three quarters and pays a quarter more. Every one of the
  forty-eight has a kind by its culture: fourteen markets, fourteen
  clanholds, ten camps and ten coves.
- **Priced by kind.** From rules 77 the hire quote names the company and
  its price by the kind, the hired company is raised of that unit and named
  for it, and the tribute a city pays is set by its kind. Realm affairs
  names a city-state's kind beside its badge; the hire quote names the
  company.
- **Save 77** upgrades v76 envelopes unchanged under the new pack; earlier
  rules read every city-state as before.

## Consequences

- Headline pacing 329 / 301 / 219 and every other course identical to rules 76; the chronicle pin at 56 holds. The headline campaigns seat no city-states, so the kinds
  cannot move them.
- A kind changes prices and companies only: no raids by a cove, no
  wandering camp, no clan feud. The wider relations of the roster remain
  unbuilt.
