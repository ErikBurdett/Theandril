# 0014 — Campaign contact and composed armies

Date: 2026-09-05. Status: implemented and verified formation/contact foundation; remaining scope recorded in IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.

The recorded Standard/Long campaign (seed 748291) reproduced exactly: victory at turn 551, no contacts or wars, despite connected land routes. Four hard-coded seats, a zero-valued exploration heuristic, and one-step movement proposals—not impassable geography—caused isolation. New-game setup exposes seat count with map-size recommendations (4/12/24/32/40). Four authored cultures remain distinct from generated seats. Explicit old counts and world generation versions remain unchanged.

AI uses only faction observations, the public movement query, and bounded frontier search. It spends the available movement budget, distributes exploration, preserves recovery and treaty constraints, and concentrates detachments into mixed forces. Campaign pace is not a movement handicap; campaign longevity must coexist with early contact and meaningful competition. Diagnostics record contact, travel utilization, wars, battles, rejected orders and save continuation, including the exact recorded seed and 32-faction Huge worlds.

Schema 6 replaces a one-unit army with a container of 1–12 stable formation IDs. Movement is shared at the slowest formation's pace; sight is the best formation's sight; upkeep and combat strength are sums. Merge, transfer and split commands are atomic, co-located, ownership-checked and cannot refresh spent movement. Formation identities and individual casualties survive reorganization and save/load. Colonist founding consumes that formation without erasing escorts. The existing tactical kernel receives the actual formations, not an invented aggregate combat unit. Canonical state contains no duplicated aggregate strength/morale fields; the detached observation supplies derived values for UI and AI.

Legacy schema-4/5 archives retain their raw origins, command rules, singleton battle IDs and hash projections. Migration alone is insufficient: archive seals must verify against the original rules/content pack. New composition commands cannot be represented as old singleton rules. New unit definitions are additive; old three unit parameters and initial army identities remain unchanged.

The UI lists real formations and offers co-located reorganization; recruitment creates a detachment that can join an army. Named characters, agents, personal magic and their missions are not represented by empty slots or unresolvable IDs: those require a later real registry and command slice. Existing reviewed sprites may gain actual consumers; no new asset is implicitly approved. Terrain overlays remain separate from approved pixels and retain physical movement information.
