# ADR 0097 — Rules/save 66: the fourth hearth-event catalogue

**Status:** accepted, 2026-10-31. Content only; no canonical field. Save 66
freezes the rules 65 pack. Evidence:
[`docs/development/2026-10-31-hearth-events-iv/`](../development/2026-10-31-hearth-events-iv/README.md).

## Context

The scope asks for one hundred and fifty hearth events; seventy-five were
sealed under rules 62, and the longer the campaign the sooner a realm meets
the same fortune twice. Every condition the catalogue reads had four or
five events after the third catalogue; the fourth keeps the spread even and
adds to the ages' own fortunes.

## Decision

- **Fifteen more templates**, across the conditions and the ages: the
  bell-founders, the tax-farmers, ash in the wells, a whale ashore, lights
  over the seam (Seams), deserters at the gate, the customs house and the
  customs dues it brings, fire in the scriptorium, the quartermaster's
  count, the peace fair (Roads), the border stones, an early winter, old
  debts called in (Bids) and the glassblowers' guild (Seams). Ninety
  templates; the rules 65 catalogue is frozen.
- **A default that commits to nothing.** The customs house's first answer,
  the one a lapsed event takes, lets the quay be; only the second raises the
  house and schedules the dues.
- **Save 66** upgrades v65 envelopes unchanged under the new pack; a v65
  envelope may not name a rules 66 template.

## Consequences

- Headline pacing under rules 66: 380 / 296 / 199; the two other seeds
  344 / 336, 276 / 266, 190 / 191; proxies 327 / 440 / 301 / 201: every headline course in band, the two other Epic seeds just under it at 344 / 336 (narrowing from the 306 / 349 recorded with the notables).
- The AI-watch chronicle journey’s pin moves 57 → 56, attributed: a draw of the larger catalogue lands on another template.
- Ninety templates against the scope's one hundred and fifty.
