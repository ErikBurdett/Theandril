# ADR 0105 — Rules/save 74: the sixth hearth-event catalogue

**Status:** accepted, 2026-11-01. Content only; no canonical field. Save 74
freezes the rules 73 pack. Evidence:
[`docs/development/2026-11-01-hearth-events-vi/`](../development/2026-11-01-hearth-events-vi/README.md).

## Context

The scope asks for one hundred and fifty hearth events; one hundred and
five were sealed under rules 73. The sixth catalogue keeps the spread even
across the conditions and the ages and brings the count within thirty of
the target.

## Decision

- **Fifteen more templates**, across the conditions and the ages: the salt
  road toll, the midwinter feast, the rubble market, the seal colony, the
  seam goes cold, the wounded column, the pilots' dispute, the ledger of
  the dead, the drill sergeant, a wedding of houses (Roads), the far survey,
  the first well, the glass scare (Seams), rival envoys (Bids) and the
  counter-envoy's return. One hundred and twenty templates; the rules 73
  catalogue is frozen.
- **A default that commits to nothing.** The rival envoys' first answer,
  the one a lapsed event takes, hears nothing; only the second sends a
  counter-envoy and schedules the return.
- **Save 74** upgrades v73 envelopes unchanged under the new pack; a v73
  envelope may not name a rules 74 template.

## Consequences

- Headline pacing under rules 74: 329 / 301 / 219; the two other seeds
  355 / 383, 286 / 315, 194 / 204; proxies 379 / 461 / 353 / 209: the headline Epic course drifts to 329, under its band, while both other Epic seeds sit in band at 355 / 383 and Long and Standard hold; the drift is recorded as one course’s, not re-priced, with the evidence below.
- The AI-watch chronicle journey’s pin at 56 holds: the Council’s turn-40 draw lands on another template under the larger catalogue, and the campaign still ends on 56.
- The headline Epic drift is a redraw, not a price: with the batch's effects
  neutralised the course runs 351, and the batch's best answers average 7.0
  by the AI's valuation against 12.1 for the first five catalogues. It is
  recorded and watched, not re-priced.
- One hundred and twenty templates against the scope's one hundred and
  fifty.
