# ADR 0116 — AI offices: a surveyor who sits as Keeper of the Roll

**Status:** accepted, 2026-11-01. AI only; no rule, save field or content
seal moves. Evidence:
[`docs/development/2026-11-01-ai-offices/`](../development/2026-11-01-ai-offices/README.md).

## Context

Offices (rules 65) gave every realm four offices and the AI a planner that
seats a vacant office's role, but only a character with no company to join
and only when the realm keeps another of that role; every headline
campaign measured since recorded that the guard never fired. The AI keeps
one surveyor and sends it to ride, so no second surveyor ever sat idle.
Legitimacy (rules 82) now counts an office held, which the AI could not
reach.

## Decision

- **A second surveyor to sit.** When the Keeper of the Roll is vacant, the
  realm already keeps a surveyor and the treasury holds two hundred coin,
  the character planner appoints a second surveyor at a hearth to sit, not
  to ride; the first stays on the road. Nothing else in the recruitment
  rule moves.
- **Seated though a company waits.** The office planner still never takes
  the realm's only character of a role, and still prefers a holder with no
  company to join; but when another of the role already rides with a
  company or is away on a mission, a sitting character with a company to
  join takes the office rather than waiting for an empty hearth.

## Consequences

- Headline pacing: 350 / 281 / 199; the two other seeds
  331 / 359, 288 / 284, 200 / 203; proxies 334 / 412 / 295 / 200. The AI-watch chronicle journey’s pin at 57 holds, no office being reached in so short a campaign; a tiny Long probe records ten offices held across three realms.
- Only the surveyor's office is sought; the Warden of the Walls, the Master
  of Works and the Reader of the Seams are still taken only when a second
  marshal, engineer or Waykeeper happens to sit idle.
