# ADR 0094 — Rules/save 63: relics of the old places

**Status:** accepted, 2026-10-31. New canonical register (`relics`), one
command, twenty-four relics of content. Save 63. Evidence:
[`docs/development/2026-10-31-relics/`](../development/2026-10-31-relics/README.md).

## Context

The scope counts relics among its content targets and the research roadmap
names artifice and relics among what magical progression still lacks. The
landmarks (rules 51 and 57) gave the world twenty-four kinds of old place,
each with a yield and a story; a relic is the thing a place keeps, and the
characters (rules 7 onward) already lead the companies they ride with by
skills that add attack and armor. A relic that adds to that leadership
needs no new battle mechanic, only a bearer.

## Decision

- **One relic for every landmark kind**, authored: the Witness Step, the
  Causeway Stone, the First Firebrick, the Boundary Stone, the Warden
  Glass, the Wreck Figurehead and the rest, each with a story and a small
  leadership bonus — up to three attack or three armor, every relic
  leading somewhere.
- **Brought up by work.** At each turn's end the first hearth of any realm
  to work a landmark's hex brings up that landmark's relic, once; the realm
  reads it in the journal. One relic per landmark on the map.
- **Borne by a character.** `bestowRelic` gives a relic of the realm to a
  living character of the realm, or takes it back; the company a bearer
  rides with gains the relic's attack and armor beside the bearer's own
  leadership. The battle snapshot freezes the relic bonus a character bore
  into the fight so the sealed record can be checked without the living
  relics.
- **Read and planned.** The observation lists the realm's relics with their
  bearers; the character sheet shows what a character bears, what waits in
  the hearth, and bestows or takes back. An AI realm bestows a waiting relic
  on the character riding with its strongest company, one a turn.
- **Save 63** appends the relics; older envelopes load with none and a
  campaign holding one cannot be sealed under earlier rules.

## Consequences

- Headline pacing under rules 63: Epic 376, Long 316, Standard 213; the two
  other seeds 390 / 365, 304 / 282, 189 / 202; proxies 336 / 440 / 290 / 197:
  every headline course of every seed in band.
- A relic has no effect beyond leadership: no sight, no yield, no rite, no
  trade; a relic cannot be lost, stolen or given to another realm, and a
  dead bearer keeps it in the register until it is taken back. Rituals,
  summons and a third magical path remain unbuilt.
