# ADR 0112 — Rules/save 80: restoration

**Status:** accepted, 2026-11-01. A rule on the unrest register; no new field,
command or content. Save 80. Evidence:
[`docs/development/2026-11-01-restoration/`](../development/2026-11-01-restoration/README.md).

## Context

The characters-and-politics roadmap asks for rebellion and secession with
valid ownership and no hidden-information leaks, and the living-campaign
roadmap for rebellion with AI response and persistence. Unrest (rules 71)
gave a hearth a voice that ends in a riot; a riot costs a household and
some lanes, and a conquered hearth riots exactly as a founded one does. The
smallest rebellion the game can state is a conquered hearth returning to
the realm that founded it, through the transfer the cession (rules 39)
already makes.

## Decision

- **The lanes rise for the old banner.** When a hearth's unrest reaches a
  hundred and the hearth is held by a realm other than its founder, with
  no army standing in it, it does not riot: it returns to its founder. The
  founder must still hold a hearth, and the hearth must not be the holder's
  capital.
- **The cession's transfer, the conquest's losses.** The hearth keeps its
  people, buildings and land as a ceded hearth does; the holder's officers
  lodged there are lost as in a conquest, its charters and musters end, its
  pending hearth event lapses, the lanes take the riot's ten devastation,
  the occupation ends and the unrest clears. Both realms read the
  restoration in their journals.
- **A garrison holds it.** Any army standing in the hearth keeps it, and
  it riots as before; lean taxes ease it as before. The holder's condition
  panel names the founder a hearth may rise for.
- **Save 80** changes no field or content; a v79 envelope loads unchanged.
  Under rules 79 the hearth riots and stays.

## Consequences

- Headline pacing under rules 80: 384 / 291 / 205; the two other seeds
  352 / 351, 289 / 282, 197 / 200; proxies 368 / 407 / 382 / 209. The AI-watch chronicle journey’s pin at 57 holds and the campaign is identical to rules 79.
- The AI holds no garrison against a restoration and does not read the
  warning; it eases taxes at sixty unrest as before, which is the only
  counterplay it has. A realm's grievance is not recorded for a hearth lost
  this way. Both are left open.
