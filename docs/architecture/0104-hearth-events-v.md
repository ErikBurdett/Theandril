# ADR 0104 — Rules/save 73: the fifth hearth-event catalogue

**Status:** accepted, 2026-11-01. Content only; no canonical field. Save 73
freezes the rules 69–72 pack. Evidence:
[`docs/development/2026-11-01-hearth-events-v/`](../development/2026-11-01-hearth-events-v/README.md).

## Context

The scope asks for one hundred and fifty hearth events; ninety were sealed
under rules 66. The catalogue's conditions and ages each had five or six
events; a fifth catalogue keeps the spread even and brings the count within
forty-five of the target.

## Decision

- **Fifteen more templates**, across the conditions and the ages: the horse
  fair, the night watch, the scorched orchards, driftwood hulls, the seam
  chanters (Seams), the levy called, fire on the quay, a visiting
  chronicler, the barracks brawl, the roads reopened (Roads), a distant
  kin's summons, the stockade, the bidder's envoy (Bids) and the envoy's
  return, the ash moths. One hundred and five templates; the rules 72
  catalogue is frozen.
- **A default that commits to nothing.** The envoy's first answer, the one a
  lapsed event takes, turns the envoy away; only the second entertains the
  envoy and schedules the return.
- **Save 73** upgrades v72 envelopes unchanged under the new pack; a v72
  envelope may not name a rules 73 template.

## Consequences

- Headline pacing under rules 73: 375 / 317 / 207; the two other seeds
  367 / 321, 290 / 266, 187 / 198; proxies 403 / 422 / 367 / 209: every headline course in band, Epic seed 20260906 back in band at 367 and seed 20260907 at 321 under it.
- The AI-watch chronicle journey’s pin at 56 holds: the Council’s turn-40 draw lands on another template under the larger catalogue, and the campaign still ends on 56.
- One hundred and five templates against the scope's one hundred and fifty.
