# ADR 0091 — Rules/save 61: pressing a city-state for tribute

**Status:** accepted, 2026-10-31. New canonical register (`demands`), one
command, no content change. Save 61. Evidence:
[`docs/development/2026-10-31-tribute-demands/`](../development/2026-10-31-tribute-demands/README.md).

## Context

M5 asks for at least one hostile or contract interaction with an independent
power beside the nonviolent ones. A city-state could be courted (rules 22),
traded with (rules 60) or conquered; nothing stood between a patron's offer
and a siege. A company at the gate of a small independent city has an older
option than either.

## Decision

- **A demand at the gate.** `demandTribute` names a city-state at peace with
  the realm, neither its patron nor its client, and a land company of the
  realm standing beside its hearth. The company's strength is weighed against
  the garrison on the walls: if it outweighs them by half again, the city
  pays what it can of a sum set by its size (twenty coin and eight a head);
  if not, the demand is refused. Either way the city remembers — its
  grievance grows and its trust falls, more when it paid — and the same
  realm cannot press it again for twenty turns. No war opens and no company
  moves.
- **The demands are canonical** from save 61, the last hundred and
  twenty-eight in order; older envelopes load with none and a campaign that
  has made one cannot be sealed under earlier rules.
- **Quoted at the gate.** The observation lists, for every own land company
  beside a city-state hearth in sight, the sum the city would pay and the
  blocker, with the realm's own demands and those made of it; Realm affairs
  shows the quote and the demand on the city-state's row; the journal
  records the payment or the refusal.
- **AI.** A realm with fewer than a hundred and fifty coin presses the
  richest open quote once a turn; a city-state pressed by a realm within
  forty turns refuses its patronage.

## Consequences

- The headline campaigns seat no city-states, so every pacing figure is
  identical to rules 60. With six seated on the headline seed, the Epic
  course sees two demands paid and four refused and the Long three and
  three; eighteen and thirteen bonds with city-states are still sworn.
- A city-state's walls are only the companies standing on its hearth; a
  demand never counts its fortifications, and a refused demand costs the
  realm nothing but the city's trust. Mercenary contracts, raiding and the
  wider roster of independent powers remain unbuilt.
