# ADR 0111 — Rules/save 79: the eighth hearth-event catalogue

**Status:** accepted, 2026-11-01. Content only; no canonical field. Save 79
freezes the rules 78 pack. Evidence:
[`docs/development/2026-11-01-hearth-events-viii/`](../development/2026-11-01-hearth-events-viii/README.md).

## Context

The scope asks for one hundred and fifty context-aware hearth events; one
hundred and thirty-five were sealed under rules 78. The eighth catalogue
keeps the spread even across the conditions and the ages and meets the
count.

## Decision

- **Fifteen more templates**, across the conditions and the ages: the road
  menders, the wards' quarrel, the salvage rights, the shipwreck's cargo,
  the seam cartel, the war levy, the harbor dredging, the copyists' strike,
  the garrison duel, the bridge toll (Roads), the far wedding, the first
  mill, the glass lanterns (Seams), the bidder's bribe (Bids) and the bribe
  discovered. One hundred and fifty templates; the rules 78 catalogue is
  frozen.
- **A default that commits to nothing.** The bidder's bribe's first answer,
  the one a lapsed event takes, refuses the bribe; only the second takes
  the coin and schedules the discovery, whose fine exceeds it.
- **Save 79** upgrades v78 envelopes unchanged under the new pack; a v78
  envelope may not name a rules 79 template, and a campaign holding one
  cannot be sealed under rules 78.

## Consequences

- Headline pacing under rules 79: 384 / 291 / 205, all in band; the two other seeds
  352 / 351, 289 / 282, 197 / 200; proxies 368 / 407 / 382 / 209 (the Long proxy bound re-derived to 450). The AI-watch chronicle journey’s pin at 57 holds, the Council’s turn-40 draw landing on another template again.
- The scope's event count is met. Every template reads the hearth, the
  realm and the age; none yet reads an office, a tax rate, a city-state
  kind or a character, which is where a ninth catalogue would go.
