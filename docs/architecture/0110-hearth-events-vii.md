# ADR 0110 — Rules/save 78: the seventh hearth-event catalogue

**Status:** accepted, 2026-11-01. Content only; no canonical field. Save 78
freezes the rules 77 pack. Evidence:
[`docs/development/2026-11-01-hearth-events-vii/`](../development/2026-11-01-hearth-events-vii/README.md).

## Context

The scope asks for one hundred and fifty hearth events; one hundred and
twenty were sealed under rules 74 and the rules 75–77 slices added none.
The seventh catalogue keeps the spread even across the conditions and the
ages and brings the count within fifteen of the target.

## Decision

- **Fifteen more templates**, across the conditions and the ages: the
  hearth census, the ale shortage, squatters in the ruins, the kelp harvest,
  fumes from the seam, the smiths pressed, the harbor light, the borrowed
  books, the garrison wedding, the market charter (Roads), the far tithe,
  the first orchard, the glass tithe (Seams), the bidder's wager (Bids) and
  the wager settled. One hundred and thirty-five templates; the rules 77
  catalogue is frozen.
- **A default that commits to nothing.** The bidder's wager's first answer,
  the one a lapsed event takes, declines the wager; only the second takes
  the coin and schedules the settling, which costs more than the coin was.
- **Save 78** upgrades v77 envelopes unchanged under the new pack; a v77
  envelope may not name a rules 78 template, and a campaign holding one
  cannot be sealed under rules 77.

## Consequences

- Headline pacing under rules 78: 339 / 281 / 213; the two other seeds
  355 / 338, 246 / 285, 194 / 199; proxies 402 / 426 / 288 / 208. The AI-watch chronicle journey’s pin moves from 56 to 57: the Council’s turn-40 draw lands on another template under the larger catalogue, attributed in the packet.
- One hundred and thirty-five templates against the scope's one hundred and
  fifty; an eighth catalogue of fifteen would meet it.
