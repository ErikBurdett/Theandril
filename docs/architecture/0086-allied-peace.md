# ADR 0086 — Rules/save 56: allied peace

**Status:** accepted, 2026-10-31. New register field (`alliances.calls`); no
new command and no content change. Save 56. Evidence:
[`docs/development/2026-10-31-allied-peace/`](../development/2026-10-31-allied-peace/README.md).

## Context

Rules 50 made a defensive pact call an ally into a war declared on its
partner, and left the ally to make its own peace. An ally dragged into a
war for its partner's sake had no way out when the partner settled; it
stood at war with a realm it had no quarrel of its own with, until it paid
for a peace or the attacker offered one. Gate G names shared peace among
what alliances lack.

## Decision

- **The call is kept.** When an ally enters a war on a call, the register
  records who answered, against whom and for whom, and the turn. The record
  stands while that war lasts.
- **The partner's peace is the ally's.** When the realm that was called for
  makes peace with the attacker — by accepting an offer, or by the peace a
  subject rule imposes — every ally still at war with that attacker on its
  call leaves the war at once, under a truce of the same term with no coin,
  its peace offers on that war lapsing; the ally, the partner and the
  attacker all read it.
- **An ally's own peace releases its call.** A call whose ally has settled
  with the attacker on its own is dropped, and the partner's later peace
  changes nothing for it.
- **The calls are public to their parties.** The observation lists the calls
  a realm answered, made or was answered against; Realm affairs says who
  entered whose war and that a peace ends it.
- **Save 56** appends the calls to the alliance register; older envelopes
  load with none, and a campaign holding a call cannot be sealed under
  earlier rules.

## Consequences

- On the Epic headline course twenty-three calls are answered and thirteen
  end with the partner's peace; on Long, twelve and eight. Headline pacing
  418 / 295 / 211, the other seeds 347 / 305, 296 / 246, 209 / 185, proxies
  353 / 468 / 323 / 204; the chronicle journey's pin at 56 holds.
- A war the ally pursues for its own reasons — a grievance, a seam, a
  siege under way — ends with its partner's peace all the same; no ally
  may stay in a war its partner has left. Guarantees of independence,
  passage rights and joint war aims remain unbuilt.
