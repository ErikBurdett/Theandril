# ADR 0102 — Rules/save 71: unrest in the hearths

**Status:** accepted, 2026-11-01. New canonical register (`unrest`), no
command, no content. Save 71. Evidence:
[`docs/development/2026-11-01-unrest/`](../development/2026-11-01-unrest/README.md).

## Context

The characters-and-politics roadmap asks for cultural unrest and the
internal consequences of how a realm is run. Taxes (rules 70) gave a realm
a lever with a price paid in devastation only; occupation and deep scars
had no voice of their own. A hearth's unrest is the smallest measure that
makes the lanes answer back.

## Decision

- **A level per hearth.** Each turn a hearth is not besieged its unrest
  rises three under heavy taxes, two while it is occupied and one while its
  devastation stands at fifty or more; with no pressure it falls one, and
  lean taxes ease it two more either way. The register holds one record
  for each hearth with unrest, by hearth; zero clears it.
- **Two consequences.** At fifty the hearth's industry is halved, after the
  rest of its yield sum; at a hundred it riots: a household leaves if more
  than one remains, the lanes take ten devastation and the unrest falls back
  to fifty, the journal recording it.
- **Read.** The observation carries each own hearth's level, pressures and
  whether its industry is halved; the hearth's condition panel shows them
  beside devastation and occupation.
- **Planned through taxes.** An AI realm sets no heavy taxes once a hearth's
  unrest reaches forty, and sets lean taxes at sixty when it holds a
  hundred and fifty coin; the rest of its tax rule stands.
- **Save 71** appends the register; older envelopes load with none and a
  campaign with any unrest cannot be sealed under earlier rules. The
  content pack is unchanged.

## Consequences

- Headline pacing under rules 71: 388 / 296 / 198; the two other seeds
  339 / 344, 283 / 286, 191 / 196; proxies 370 / 478 / 305 / 207: every headline course in band and identical to rules 70, the two other Epic seeds just under it.
- The AI-watch chronicle journey’s pin at 56 holds and the headline campaign is identical to rules 70; two courses on the other seeds move as their AI realms answer the lanes.
- Unrest knows three pressures and two consequences: no culture of the
  founder against the ruler, no legitimacy, no secession, no rebels on the
  map. Rulers, offices of state and the wider political cast remain unbuilt.
