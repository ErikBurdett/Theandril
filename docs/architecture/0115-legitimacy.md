# ADR 0115 — Rules/save 82: legitimacy

**Status:** accepted, 2026-11-01. A derived reading of the realm; no
canonical field, command or content. Save 82. Evidence:
[`docs/development/2026-11-01-legitimacy/`](../development/2026-11-01-legitimacy/README.md).

## Context

The scope lists legitimacy and authority among the politics of a realm, and
the characters-and-politics roadmap asks for legitimacy and
internal-interest consequences at strategic scope. Offices (rules 65),
clients (rules 21 and 39), conquests and the capital were facts with no
common reading; unrest (rules 71) and restoration (rules 80) gave the lanes
a voice but nothing of the realm's standing spoke to it.

## Decision

- **Read from the world.** Every realm starts settled at two. An office of
  the realm held adds one and a client answering to it adds one; a seat
  that is another realm's hearth, or none, takes one, as does holding more
  hearths of other realms' founding than of its own, and answering to a
  patron. The level is never below zero. No save field: the reading is the
  same for every reader of the same state.
- **Weighs on the lanes.** At one or less the realm's legitimacy is weak
  and every hearth's unrest rises one more each turn, named among its
  pressures; at three or more it is strong and every hearth's unrest falls
  one more. Settled legitimacy changes nothing.
- **Read in Realm affairs** with its level, its standing and every reason.
- **Save 82** changes no field or content; a v81 envelope loads unchanged.
  Under rules 81 the reading does not exist and the lanes answer only their
  own pressures.

## Consequences

- Headline pacing under rules 82: 384 / 291 / 205; the two other seeds
  352 / 351, 289 / 282, 197 / 200; proxies 388 / 407 / 382 / 209. The AI-watch chronicle journey’s pin at 57 holds and the campaign is identical to rules 81.
- The AI holds no office, so its legitimacy is settled at best and weak as
  a client or a conqueror; it answers only through lean taxes. Succession,
  loyalty and rulers remain open.
