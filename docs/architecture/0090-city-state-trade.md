# ADR 0090 — Rules/save 60: the goods a client city-state sends

**Status:** accepted, 2026-10-31. Content only; no canonical field. Save 60
freezes the rules 59 pack. Evidence:
[`docs/development/2026-10-31-trade-goods/`](../development/2026-10-31-trade-goods/README.md).

## Context

The twenty-four city-states (rules 21) differ in name, colour, motto and
history, and a client city-state (rules 22) differs from a client realm in
nothing but its single hearth. M5 asks for trade communities with distinct
trade benefits, and the resource economy (eight goods, stockpiles, material
costs) already gives a realm reasons to want a good it lacks.

## Decision

- **Every city-state trades in one good of its country**, authored in a
  table of twenty-four: iron from Bellowstead's kilns, salt from Dunwell's
  cisterns, horses from Grasshalt, ashglass from Cullethaven and Chalkvigil,
  silver from Sixways, Lodestair, Hollowlease and Duskpress, and so on,
  one to two a turn. The table seals into the pack from rules 60.
- **The good comes with the tribute.** Each turn a client city-state pays
  its tribute, its good goes to the patron's stockpile; a missed tribute
  sends nothing. The bond's swearing, by acceptance or under a subject rule,
  says what will come.
- **It is read.** The observation lists the goods between a realm and its
  clients or patron; the patron's oath line in Realm affairs names them.
- **The AI courts the good it lacks.** Among the city-states a realm might
  take as clients, one whose good the realm holds fewer than six of is
  courted first; nothing is guessed beyond the quoted trade and the realm's
  own stockpile.
- **Save 60** upgrades v59 envelopes unchanged under the new pack.

## Consequences

- The headline campaigns seat no city-states, so every pacing figure is
  identical to rules 59 (395 / 302 / 203; 338 / 338, 268 / 287, 185 / 199;
  proxies 307 / 431 / 302 / 181). With six city-states seated on the Epic
  headline seed, thirty bonds with city-states are sworn over the campaign
  and it ends on 391.
- A realm client trades nothing; a good is a flat amount, not a share of
  the city-state's works; there is no market, no price and no trade
  between realms. The wider roster of independent powers remains unbuilt.
