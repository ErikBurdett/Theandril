# ADR 0089 — Rules/save 59: regional crises

**Status:** accepted, 2026-10-31. Content and one optional field on the
standing crisis. Save 59 freezes the rules 57–58 pack. Evidence:
[`docs/development/2026-10-31-regional-crises/`](../development/2026-10-31-regional-crises/README.md).

## Context

Rules 53 made every crisis world-wide and identical for every realm: the
same toll, the same quote, the same choice everywhere. The roadmap kept
"regional crises" as accepted scope without a system, and a world whose
every crisis falls on everyone at once has no geography to it.

## Decision

- **A regional crisis has a centre and a radius.** One for each later age
  — the Valley Blight (Roads, ten hexes, food), the Glass Storm (Seams, ten
  hexes, devastation), the Bread Riots (Bids, twelve hexes, coin) — joins
  the age's pool from rules 59, drawn from the same seed as the rest. Its
  centre is one of the world's hearths, drawn from the seed as the crisis
  begins; a world without a hearth draws from the world-wide crises.
- **It reaches only the hearths within its radius.** The toll falls on
  those hearths alone; the shelter quote counts them alone and a realm with
  none within it cannot shelter and reads why; the crisis's event comes to
  a hearth it reaches; the migration it leaves behind moves households from
  the hearths it reached. The observation carries the centre, the radius,
  the hearth it is centred on and the realm's own hearths within it, and
  the Realm affairs card says how many of the realm's hearths lie within.
- **The AI counts only reached hearths** at risk of migration.
- **Save 59** adds the optional centre to the standing crisis; a campaign
  with a regional crisis standing cannot be sealed under earlier rules, and
  older envelopes load unchanged. Each regional crisis has its own hearth
  event; sixty templates.

## Consequences

- Headline pacing under rules 59: Epic 395, Long 302, Standard 203, with
  the other seeds 338 / 338, 268 / 287, 185 / 199 and proxies
  307 / 431 / 302 / 181: every headline course inside its band. The Epic
  headline draws the Valley Blight and the Glass Storm; seed 20260906 the
  Valley Blight and the Tremors; seed 20260907 the Toll Wars and the Glass
  Fever. The chronicle journey's pin at 54 holds.
- A regional crisis is centred on a hearth, never on a seam, a landmark or a
  coast; its radius is fixed per crisis; collapse remains unbuilt.
