# 0061 — Seam study: a path to the third victory for every culture

Rules/save **39** (shared with subject rule, cession and ground in sight). The
third victory path needs a Waykeeper of the second degree. Four cultures are born
to it; every other culture reaches it only by deepening a path for 24 earned
experience, and Waykeepers earned experience from battle alone. A realm at peace
could never qualify, and the AI never appointed a Waykeeper outside a war.

## Rule

A living, unwounded Waykeeper with no mission, **lodged in a hearth whose borders
hold a surveyed seam**, gains one experience each turn in the character phase
(`SEAM_STUDY_EXPERIENCE`). A caster riding with an army, wounded or on a mission
does not study; a hearth that loses its seam stops the study. The seam hearths
are computed once a turn from the world's few seams, so the cost is bounded by
seams times realms. Historical rules grant nothing.

## AI

A realm at peace that holds a seam of every path and has a hearth able to host a
working appoints a Waykeeper there and keeps it lodged, declining the usual
attachment to a co-located battle army while no war calls for it. From there the
existing planners carry on: deepening when the experience is earned, uncastable
Arcane Theory once every path is held and a caster of the second degree serves,
and the sealed working once every requirement is met.

## Compatibility

No save field changes. Experience was already canonical and bounded. The rule is
gated on rules 39; older campaigns and historical execution are unchanged.

## Limits

Study is one experience a turn: a non-tradition culture needs twenty-four lodged
turns before a path deepens. Only one Waykeeper is appointed for study, and only
in peace; a realm at war still appoints for its battle armies first.
