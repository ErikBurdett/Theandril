## An answer to a working

Until now a working could only be met with steel. Unbinding changes that. It is cast at one enemy formation on the field and strips the binding from it: any ward is undone, and every Waykeeper riding with that formation is jarred badly enough to lose the coming round. It costs five strain and can be used once in a battle, so it is a decision about when the other side has committed, not something to lead with.

It also needs something to undo. Aimed at a formation that carries neither a ward nor a caster, Unbinding refuses and tells you why — *nothing binds this formation* — rather than burning the one use you had.

The bigger change is who can cast it. A Waykeeper now trains in the tradition of the culture that appointed them. The Cinder March and the Emberwake Convocation reach the second degree of Flame; the Sepulchral Synod and the Margin Observance reach the second degree of Rune. Only the second degree of Rune can unbind, so two realms in the world can answer a working and the rest cannot — they have to beat it another way. A realm also studies theory inside its own tradition at three quarters of the usual price, so what your culture is good at is cheaper as well as reachable.

Automatic casting plays the same board: a counter goes to the strongest binding on the field, a ward to the front rank, and a damaging working to whichever enemy is nearest to breaking.
