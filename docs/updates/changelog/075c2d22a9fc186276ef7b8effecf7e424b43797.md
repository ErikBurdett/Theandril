## What actually wants you

Two numbers at the bottom of the screen used to be the whole story: so many armies needing orders, so many idle settlements. They told you how much work was waiting and nothing at all about what kind, and the only way through was to press Next until you had seen all of it.

Now the list tells you its shape. **What wants a decision** breaks the totals into the reasons behind them, largest first — a hundred companies with movement remaining, forty hearths with an empty production queue, three companies with an interrupted route.

And each line is its own queue. Click the interrupted routes and you go straight to the first one; Next and Previous then walk only those three, not the hundred. Finding the handful of things that have actually gone wrong no longer means visiting everything that hasn't.

Between this and the hearth charters and army postings, a wide realm's end of turn is finally about exceptions. What answers for itself stays quiet; what is stuck says so, and says why.
