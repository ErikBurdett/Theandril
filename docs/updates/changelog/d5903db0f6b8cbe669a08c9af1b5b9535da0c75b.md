## Companies that know where to go

Raise a company and it used to arrive as its own little army, standing in the hearth that built it, waiting for you to walk it across the map and fold it into the force it was always meant to join. Six hearths raising troops meant six of those walks, every time.

Now an army can be posted. Name a hex it has explored and it marches there on its own, under exactly the travel order you would have given it — the same roads, the same fog, the same halt when something hostile comes into view. When it arrives it either holds the hex or joins the force standing on it, folding in the moment it gets there.

A posting never argues with you. It only moves an army that has no travel order of its own, so anything you order yourself happens first and the posting waits its turn. Clear it and the army is back under direct orders, with nothing to undo.

The part that saves the most clicking is the muster point. Give a hearth one, and every company it raises is posted there the turn it forms and joins whatever it finds. Set it once at the start of a war and the reinforcements simply arrive.

And a posting tells you where it stands: marching, standing, or stuck — and if it is stuck, why. There is no known route. There is nothing here to join. The road was interrupted. A posted army that is getting on with it drops out of your end-of-turn list entirely; a stuck one shows up with its reason attached. Between this and the hearth charters, what is left in that list is the things that actually want you.
