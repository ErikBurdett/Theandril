## What the Ashfall left behind

Magic now begins with ground. The Ashfall left arcane seams buried under the vitrified glass it scattered, and where those seams lie is a property of the world itself — the same for every realm that plays it, and hidden from all of them until someone goes looking.

Any company can stop and survey the ground it stands on for twenty-four coin. The survey spends that company's movement and reveals any seam within two hexes, to your realm alone; a survey that finds nothing says so and still costs. Ashfall glass is visible to everyone, so you can see where it is worth looking without being told where the seams are.

A seam inside your borders draws ashglass every turn, and falls to whoever takes the hearth that holds it. And a held seam is now what allows a realm to study Arcane Theory at all: research alone no longer reaches magic. You have to find the ground, take it, and keep it.

Rival realms do the same. A realm holding no seam will survey its own hinterland from time to time, and will reach for a seam it has found when its borders grow.
