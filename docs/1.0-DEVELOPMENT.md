## Selection and completion contract

1. Reproduce a ranked audit finding against current code before implementing it.
   Capture source revision, exact commands and a fast failing regression.
2. Repair save/command safety before adding systems that depend on it. R01 battle
   morale, R02 complete history persistence and R03 contestable large stacks are
   the first foundation. Independent consumer/UI defects can proceed in parallel
   with explicit non-overlapping file ownership.
3. Preserve an independently captured old-rules fixture before changing outcomes.
   Separate campaign rules, tactical rules, save schema and archive format versions.
   Never bless newly regenerated hashes as evidence of historical compatibility.
4. Implement a vertical slice: canonical rules, affordable costs and counterplay,
   filtered observations, human controls, ordinary AI commands, save/load,
   deterministic continuation/replay, tests and truthful status documentation.
5. Verify each accepted command boundary, not only end turns. Temporary battle
   effects must not become permanent strategic stats by copying result fields.
   Mid-battle, immediate aftermath, repeated combat, capture and retreat all count.
6. Distinguish resolved issues from partial slices. Preserve failing release gates;
   do not alter contact thresholds, suppress AI or raise size/time limits just to
   obtain a green result. Record diagnostic/harness failures separately.

## Design defaults when the brief leaves room

Borrow design principles, not names, prose, artwork or complete subsystems:

- **Total War: Warhammer III:** distinctive army roles, legible battlefield intent,
  terrain/composition/leadership tradeoffs, strategic consequences and counterplay.
- **Civilization:** understandable opportunity costs, useful empire-level management,
  visible progress toward genuinely different, contestable victory paths.
- **Songs of Syx:** households, production, logistics and shortages that explain
  themselves at settlement and realm scale; depth should reduce tedious clicking.
- **Stellaris:** purposeful long-term goals, differentiated societies, escalation and
  late-campaign decisions rather than multiplied waiting costs.
- **Europa Universalis:** durable diplomatic/war objectives, credible commitments,
  geography and logistics; expose consequences before the player commits.

These are direction-setting references, not claims of fresh competitor playtests.
Translate them into Theandril's hearths, cultures, terrain, doctrine and magic.
A named inspiration is not permission for an enormous rewrite or another 1.0 gate.
Prefer a small coherent playable loop with AI counterplay to a catalog of buttons.

## Art and factory handoff

Read `docs/art/README.md` and the game-specific art skills before production. Locate
the actual factories via the workstation project map; do not assume the shorthand
`/projects/BlenderArtFactory` or `/pixel-art-factory` exists. Inspect each factory's
current README/doctor and supported adapter rather than inferring functionality.

Audit consumer routing before commissioning art: Waykeeper's missing display was
an early-return binding defect despite a published shared caster. Existing battle
sets contain real action frames; a contact sheet is not playback approval. Preserve
reviewed assets, editable sources and hashes. `paf pixelate` processes pixels; it
does not author missing silhouettes or generate original imagery. Background
Blender can work without a connected interactive MCP scene. Keep factory review,
actual pixel inspection and engine acceptance as separate evidence.

## Verification and evidence

Use the repository package scripts and installed runtime; report actual versions.
For an integration checkpoint run typecheck, lint, content validation, affected
unit/property/replay tests, the full suite and a production build. Exercise changed
human workflows in Playwright and inspect screenshots at normal/narrow scales.
Measure affected large workloads without presenting concurrent or software-GPU
samples as isolated physical-hardware certification.

Retain new evidence under `docs/development/` or the repository's existing
architecture/performance/review directories. Reference the original Rxx IDs and
record: reproduction, design choice, touched boundaries, exact checks and exits,
compatibility, known limits and next dependency-ready player outcome. Do not mix
new results into the historical audit's raw logs or checksum manifests.
