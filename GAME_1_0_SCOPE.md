# Theandril 1.0 Feature Scope

This is a release scope, not a brainstorm. A system counts only when it is playable, integrated, AI-aware where applicable, saved, and tested.

## Core campaign

- Procedural giant world with multiple continents and islands.
- Seeded deterministic generation.
- 24+ authored major factions.
- 48+ minor/independent powers or equivalent varied generated actors.
- Single-player campaigns.
- Online 2–8 human campaigns with AI seats.
- Multiple map sizes through Legendary stress scale.
- Multiple victory paths.
- Campaign epoch progression and world crises.
- Save/load/autosave/export/import/replay.
- Campaigns intended to last hundreds of turns, potentially around 1,000 at higher difficulties; short test/skirmish pacing must not become the default 1.0 experience.
- After victory in an AI campaign, a complete technical record and a factual historical tome, both preserved across saves and viewable/downloadable from the UI.

## Exploration

- Fog of war and current visibility.
- Scouting and naval exploration.
- Terrain movement.
- Select a unit, then click a reachable hex to move or explicitly attack a hostile force; preview movement costs and paths.
- Saved multi-turn travel and waypoints with visible interruption reasons, cancellation and deliberate resume after blockers, faction actions or events.
- Coherent biome diversity with readable terrain/biome distinctions.
- Authored faction-biome affinities and paid, saved cultivation with scaling costs; terrain changes remain fog-filtered and preserve movement/battle-history integrity.
- Discoveries, ruins, lairs, landmarks.
- Resource discovery.
- Map search, minimap, filters/lenses.
- Intelligence sharing/trading where diplomacy allows.

## Progression

Theandril 1.0 requires multiple different advancement systems rather than one universal tree:

### Mundane Technology / Knowledge
- 80+ discoveries.
- Agriculture, metallurgy, engineering, navigation, logistics, medicine, administration, construction, economy, and other practical branches.
- Equipment/building/infrastructure/unit unlocks.

### Institutions / Civic Traditions
- 70+ institutions/traditions.
- Government, law, vassalage, economy, social organization, scholarship, diplomacy, religion, and administration.
- Adoption/reform/spread mechanics where appropriate.

### Military Doctrine
- 50+ doctrine discoveries.
- Formation, logistics, siege, cavalry, skirmishing, naval, magical warfare, monster warfare, officer corps, raiding, and faction-specific doctrines.

### Arcane Theory
- 8–10 original research disciplines.
- National research knowledge is distinct from personal caster capability.
- Research queues and specialization.

### Magical Traditions / Paths
- 10–14 broad original magical traditions/paths.
- Individual casters possess different aptitudes/levels.
- Factions have asymmetric access.
- Some effects require multiple traditions.

### Battle Magic
- Buffs/debuffs.
- Damage/control.
- Terrain/weather effects.
- Summons.
- Wards/counter-magic.
- Morale/illusion effects.
- Formation-aware casting.
- AI scripting/selection.

### Strategic Rituals
- Summoning.
- Site searching.
- Divination/intelligence.
- Settlement protection/curses.
- Movement/travel.
- Terrain/world manipulation.
- Magical infrastructure.
- Faction-wide/world effects.
- Counter-rituals/dispelling.

### Magical Geography
- Hidden sites.
- Ley regions.
- haunted/holy/arcane landmarks.
- faction-specific site interactions.
- magical resource extraction.
- site searching and discovery.

### Artifice / Relics
- 80+ craftable/discoverable magical items, recipes, and relics.
- Character equipment and army/settlement artifacts.
- Rare one-of-a-kind relics.

### Sacred Systems
- Temples/shrines.
- Priestly hierarchy.
- sacred sites.
- blessings/rites.
- consecration/exorcism.
- faction/religion-specific rules.

### Forbidden / Occult Systems
- Powerful research and rituals with real strategic costs.
- Corruption, sacrifice, instability, dangerous summons, mutations, or world-threat escalation where appropriate.
- Counterplay.

### Cross-System Discoveries
- Technology + magic combinations.
- Institutions + magic.
- Military doctrine + magic.
- Geography/site-gated research.
- Character-gated breakthroughs.

### General progression requirements
- Faction affinities.
- Faction-exclusive discoveries.
- Ancient/ruin discoveries.
- Espionage/theft.
- Event breakthroughs.
- catch-up mechanisms.
- AI-aware research planning.
- "who can use this?" UI for magical unlocks.
