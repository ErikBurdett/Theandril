# Provider Bake-off

Do not pick a provider based on reputation alone.

Generate comparable:
- armored infantry;
- mage;
- non-human;
- large monster;
- terrain;
- settlement;
- short animation.

Score:
- art quality;
- pixel correctness;
- style consistency;
- animation identity;
- controllability;
- automation;
- latency;
- cost;
- reproducibility;
- provenance/license clarity.

Document in:
`docs/art/GENERATOR_BAKEOFF.md`

Different providers may win different asset classes.
