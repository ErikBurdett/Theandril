Realms bound by oath, treaty or a supply agreement are never pursued. Headline pacing is unchanged at 368 / 300 / 203.

AI realms now hold the seams they find: a seam within four hexes of a hearth is claimed toward one legal hex at a time, and a seam farther away in nobody's borders is settled beside by a free caravan given the road, or by one raised at the nearest hearth once the purse covers the whole new hearth and a company can quote the road. At the end of the Epic headline campaign every found seam is held and eight of twelve realms hold a path, against three before.
