# Theandril Pixel-Art Style Standard

## Core feel

Premium dark-fantasy strategy art rendered as intentional pixel art.

The world should feel:
- old;
- dangerous;
- inhabited;
- political;
- magical;
- weathered;
- geographically distinct.

## Lighting

Default material lighting:
- primary light from upper-left / northwest;
- restrained highlights;
- deep but readable shadows.

Magic may override lighting locally.

## Outlines

Use selective outlines.

Avoid:
- universal black sticker borders;
- overly soft edges.

Prefer darker local-color outlines where appropriate.

## Materials

### Iron / steel
Cool dark shadows, restrained bright highlights.

### Bronze / copper
Warm oxidized shadows and selective warm accents.

### Leather
Broad warm-value shapes, limited noisy texture.

### Cloth
Faction-readable color blocks first, folds second.

### Stone
Large clusters; avoid salt-and-pepper texture.

### Wood
Visible grain only where resolution supports it.

## Terrain

At strategic zoom:
- silhouette and color grouping matter more than tiny detail;
- biome identity should be obvious;
- borders/roads/rivers must remain readable;
- terrain should not fight faction colors.

## Units

At 64px:
- head/helmet;
- torso;
- weapon;
- shield/off-hand;
- faction accent

must read immediately.

## Magic

Use constrained luminous ramps.

Magic should stand out because most of the world is comparatively subdued.

## Faction art families

Every major faction needs:
- silhouette grammar;
- material grammar;
- palette accents;
- heraldic shapes;
- armor motifs;
- architectural motifs;
- magic motifs.

## UI

Pixel art may be used for icons, ornaments, items, and faction crests.

Do not force body text into pixel fonts if readability suffers.
